## Docs are content again

The reasoning for moving [Logixlysia](/projects/logixlysia) documentation from Fumadocs to Blume, and
what changes when documentation is treated as content with its own lifecycle instead of as an artefact of
the application framework that happens to render it.

## Stop trusting events

The most portable idea in the set, and the one that reaches furthest outside its examples. React diffs
the DOM; Kubernetes diffs reality. Both systems work because they compare desired state against observed
state and converge, rather than trusting a stream of events to have been delivered, ordered, and
processed exactly once. The post argues reconciliation as a general mental model, with the
[GitOps load balancer](/projects/kubernetes-automated-load-balancer) as the worked case.
