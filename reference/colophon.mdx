## What this site is

A reference edition of [pungrumpy.com](https://pungrumpy.com), published at
`pungrumpy.mintlify.app`. The portfolio is the canonical source and holds the screenshots and the
marketing copy. This site holds the facts in a form that is quick to scan, easy to link into, and
cheap for an agent to read whole.

It also keeps the `pungrumpy.mintlify.app` subdomain in active use rather than idle.

## How it is built

| Layer | Choice |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | Mintlify, configured through `docs.json` |
| Source | [github.com/PunGrumpy/docs](https://github.com/PunGrumpy/docs) |
| Typeface | Geist for headings and body |
| Palette | Monochrome, near-black `#0A0A0A` on white, inverted for dark |
| Appearance | Follows the reader's system setting, with no visible theme control |
| Custom CSS | One small `style.css` for measure, tabular numerals, and table alignment |

## Design rules

The composition follows the [Vercel brand design guidelines](https://vercel.com/design.md). The
rules that shaped the most decisions here:

**Clarity over decoration.** Confidence comes from clarity, proof, and command of the material, not from
hype or ornament. Every page states what it knows and links the source.

**Design in monochrome.** Colour is added only when it encodes meaning, paired with a non-colour cue.
Nothing on this site is coloured because it is favourable.

**Prefer spacing and typography to borders and boxes.** The page is one continuous canvas. Surfaces are
earned by real grouping, not applied by default, which is why there are no card grids or metric
tiles here.

**Tables for lookup, prose for conclusions.** Facts that a reader scans for, such as dates, issuers, and
URLs, are tabulated at full width with headers aligned to their columns. Anything that requires an
argument is written as prose in a readable measure.

**Sentence case, concrete nouns, no decorative labels.** No all-caps eyebrows, no badges or pills around
ordinary metadata, no em dashes in headings.

**Default to stillness.** No scroll reveals, no hover motion, no decorative animation, and reduced-motion
preferences respected.

## Accessibility

One descriptive heading per page in document order, semantic tables with real headers, link text
that reads on its own, visible focus, and equivalent hierarchy in light and dark. Nothing on this
site communicates through colour alone.
