## Why a lab and not a component library

A component library is a promise: it has an API, a version, and users who will be annoyed when it
changes. A lab has none of those, which is precisely what makes the experiments worth doing. Ideas get
built badly first, and the ones that survive contact with a real project graduate into
[Verno Studio](/projects/verno-studio) or a [client site](/projects/client-work) with their rough edges
already found.
