## What a bootstrap CLI is actually for

The value is not in saving the twenty minutes of `npm install`. It is in the decisions that a template
freezes: linting, formatting, testing, CI, directory layout, and the small conventions that are cheap to
set on day one and expensive to change in month six. A studio that ships several projects a year needs
those decisions made once and applied identically, or every project drifts into its own dialect.

That is the design engineering claim in practice. The tooling and the interface come from the same
judgement, and the CLI is where the judgement is stored.
