Thai Seatizen is a gamified marine life education platform powered by AI, changing how people learn about
and engage with Thailand's underwater biodiversity.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Year | 2025 |
| Client | IBM Thailand |
| Role | Full-stack development, DevOps |
| Coverage | [LinkedIn announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/anothaiwettayakorn_ibm-thailand-watsonx-ugcPost-7386232984665804800-dJlO) |
| Showcase | [pungrumpy.com/showcase/thai-seatizen](https://www.pungrumpy.com/showcase/thai-seatizen) |

The project shipped with a public landing surface, an AI chatbot for learner questions, and an MOU
signing between the partners. It ran during the IBM Thailand platform engineering engagement described
under [Experience](/experience), which is why the DevOps half of the work is not incidental to it: the
same person built the deployment path and the product on top of it.
