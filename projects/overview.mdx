infrastructure, and design engineering for people who needed a site rather than a product.

## How to read the years

2023 produced one thing and kept it alive: Logixlysia is the longest-running project here and the most
depended upon. 2024 and 2025 are infrastructure years, when the platform work at KMITL and IBM was
setting the agenda. 2026 is a tooling year: four independent developer tools shipped, each one a command
that was being retyped often enough to justify a binary.
