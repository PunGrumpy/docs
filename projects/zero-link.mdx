## Why build one at all

Every engineer builds a link shortener eventually, and the reason it stays a useful exercise is that the
problem is entirely about the parts nobody demos: redirect latency, identifier collisions, and what
happens to a link after the person who made it has forgotten it exists. It is a small enough surface that
those questions cannot hide behind features.
