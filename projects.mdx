history: developer tools that remove a repeated manual step, platform work that came out of running real
infrastructure, and design engineering for people who needed a site rather than a product.

Each entry links to its full write-up and screenshots on
[pungrumpy.com/showcase](https://www.pungrumpy.com/showcase), which is the canonical record.

## How to read the years

2023 produced one thing and kept it alive. Logixlysia is the longest-running project here and the most
depended upon, at 264 GitHub stars as of August 2026, and it is the only one with its own documentation
site.

2024 and 2025 are infrastructure years, when the platform work at KMITL and IBM Thailand was setting the
agenda: GitOps load balancing, metadata validation, and the Thai Seatizen build.

2026 is a tooling year. Four independent developer tools shipped, each one a command that was being
retyped often enough to justify a binary, alongside three client portfolios and the studio that makes
shipping them practical.
