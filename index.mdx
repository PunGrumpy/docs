Noppakorn Kaewsalabnil, who publishes as PunGrumpy, is a software engineer in Bangkok working across
DevSecOps, platform engineering, and design engineering. The work divides into two halves that keep
meeting: infrastructure that should be secure and reliable by default, and interfaces that should
feel considered rather than merely functional.

This site is the reference edition of [pungrumpy.com](https://pungrumpy.com). The portfolio presents
the work; this documents it, in a shape that a person scanning for one fact and an agent reading the
whole corpus can both use.

## The current state of the work

Fourteen projects are published on the showcase, spanning 2023 to 2026. The most used of them is
[Logixlysia](https://www.pungrumpy.com/showcase/logixlysia), a logging plugin for ElysiaJS with 264
GitHub stars as of August 2026 and its own documentation site. The most recent are a run of
developer tools shipped through 2026: [Docker
Doctor](https://www.pungrumpy.com/showcase/docker-doctor),
[Pigo](https://www.pungrumpy.com/showcase/pigo), [Better
Commit](https://www.pungrumpy.com/showcase/better-commit), and [Git
Bench](https://www.pungrumpy.com/showcase/git-bench).

Alongside the tools sit platform projects that came out of real operations rather than side
interest: a [GitOps load balancer on
Kubernetes](https://www.pungrumpy.com/showcase/kubernetes-automated-load-balancer) and [Thai
Seatizen](https://www.pungrumpy.com/showcase/thai-seatizen), an AI marine-education platform built
with IBM Thailand.
